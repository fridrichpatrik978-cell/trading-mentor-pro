# Trading Mentor PRO

Trading Mentor PRO is an advanced desktop application designed for financial market traders. It serves as an intelligent assistant and personal mentor, helping traders build discipline and protect their capital from emotional risk.

### Key Features:
* **Trading Journal with MetaTrader 5 Import:** Easily import your MT5 trade history, attach chart screenshots, and add custom notes or descriptions to your market analyses.
* **Advanced Trading Statistics:** Automatically generates detailed performance metrics, win-rate analytics, and position management statistics.
* **Risk Management & Anti-Gambling Protection:** The system acts as an active mentor—analyzing trading behavior, flagging rule violations, and warning you immediately if your trading starts shifting into emotional gambling instead of a structured strategy.
